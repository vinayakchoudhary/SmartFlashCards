Why Smart Flash Cards?

Smart Flash Cards help you remember facts and concepts more and for longer with the help of spaced repetition, automatically deciding which cards need to be revised each day, maximising memory which minimising time and efford

How to use smart flash cards?

You need to enter concept or facts you want to remember in question and answers formal into the add cards page, each flash card can either require you to type out the answer (for factual information like years, name etc) or will ask you if you knew the answer after it's revealed (for more conception questions)

How it works?

Each flash card you make is give a level, and that level decided how often it's revise. The levels are:

  • Level - 1 : To be revised everyday, every card starts here, getting a flash card right once sends it to level - 2
  • Level - 2 : To be revised every 2 days, getting a flash card right 2 consecutive time sends it to level - 3, not knowing an answer once sends it to level - 1
  • Level - 3 : To be revised every week, getting a flash card right 3 consecutive time sends it to level - 4, not knowing an answer once sends it to level - 2
  • Level - 4 : To be revised every two week, getting a flash card right 4 consecutive time sends it to level - 5, not knowing an answer once sends it to level - 3
  • Level - 5 : To be revised every month, not knowing an answer once sends it to level - 4

If you ever forget to complete your flash cards, the skipped flash cards would have their level dropped by 1 or 2 and they will be rescheduled over the next 7 days to minimised the extra load while ensuring you don't forget them.
